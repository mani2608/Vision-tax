Built for Business Owners Who Wear Many Hats

Accurate books are how you know real cash flow, profitability, and what you can legally pay yourself. For LLCs, S-Corps, and growing IT consultancies, consistent monthly bookkeeping also eliminates IRS audit flags and makes business tax returns faster, cleaner, and less expensive.

Vision-Tax establishes a disciplined monthly rhythm: we import and categorize transactions, reconcile bank and credit accounts, produce financial statements you can actually understand, and flag tax issues before they turn into year-end emergencies.

Phase 1

Built for busy founders and consultants

If you are an IT consultant, technical contractor, or small business founder, bookkeeping often falls behind while you focus on client deliverables. We catch up messy records when needed, then maintain a hands-off monthly routine so lender requests, estimated taxes, and payroll run smoothly.

Phase 2

What you receive each month

Every month you receive complete transaction reconciliation, transparent expense breakdowns, owner compensation tracking (salary vs distributions), and clean financial records prepared for federal and state tax filings.

Phase 3

Tax-ready all year round

When books stay clean, annual return preparation is completed in days rather than weeks, every allowable deduction is supported by documentation, and quarterly estimated tax planning becomes predictable.
