Common IRS Notices We Resolve Every Week

In over 80% of automated CP2000 underreported income notices, the proposed tax bill is completely incorrect because the IRS lacked cost-basis information for RSU or stock sales on Form 1099-B.

  • CP2000 (Proposed Changes): Typically triggered by automated computer matching of W-2, 1099, or crypto reports.
  • CP501 / CP503 / CP504 (Balance Due Notices): Demands for payment where penalties and interest have compounded.
  • Letter 525 / Audit Inquiries: Requests for substantiation of itemized deductions, home office, or travel expenses.
  • Foreign Asset (FBAR) Inquiries: Resolving FinCEN compliance inquiries and voluntary disclosure procedures.
