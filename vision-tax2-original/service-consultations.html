Why Unlimited CPA Tax Consultations?

Many filers only speak to their tax preparer right before the filing deadline, when it is already too late to change their tax outcome for the prior year. Our unlimited advisory service empowers you to make proactive financial decisions in real time.

  • Job Changes & W-4 Tuning: Adjust your withholdings immediately to avoid paying underpayment interest or leaving thousands on the table.
  • Marriage & Family Filing Elections: Analyze Married Filing Jointly vs Separately to minimize overall tax liability and maximize child tax credits.
  • Side Hustles & 1099 Income: Learn what legitimate business expenses you can deduct, from home office to software subscriptions.
  • Real Estate & Rental Properties: Understand depreciation schedules, mortgage interest caps, and state property tax limits.
