Combining Strategic Planning with Accurate Filing

Business tax preparation should accurately reflect how your company operates: contractor income, vendor expenses, multi-state activity, owner distributions, and tax elections already in place. Guessing late creates severe underpayment penalties and missed planning opportunities.

Vision-Tax pairs proactive tax planning with return preparation — so estimated taxes, deductions, owner compensation, and filing deadlines are managed as one seamless workflow for US small business owners, IT practices, and startups.

Phase 1

Entities we commonly support

Single-member and multi-member LLCs, S-Corporations, partnerships, and C-Corporations that connect to owner personal returns. If your consulting firm operates across multiple states or ownership percentages are changing, we map the exact filing picture.

Phase 2

Planning that directly affects the return

Before year-end we review financial projections, quarterly estimated tax pacing, retirement contribution timing (SEP-IRA, Solo 401k), Section 179 equipment purchases, and owner compensation questions that directly lower what you owe.

Phase 3

What preparation delivers

Organized collection of business records, return preparation with draft review, federal and applicable state filings, coordination with bookkeeping when available, and clear next steps for payments, extensions, and the upcoming tax year.

  • S-Corp W-2 Salary Optimization: Establish IRS-compliant reasonable compensation to shield thousands in self-employment taxes.
  • Foreign-Owned LLC Compliance: Timely Form 5472 and pro-forma 1120 filing to protect against $25,000 IRS late penalties.
  • Direct IRS e-File Confirmation: Electronic filing with official IRS submission acceptance timestamps.
