Comprehensive Federal & Multi-State 1040 Tax Filing

Navigating US tax returns can be intricate, particularly when dealing with dual status, foreign income, stock compensations (RSUs, ESPP), or moving between states. Vision-Tax delivers certified CPA preparation tailored to your exact tax situation.

  • Form 1040 (Resident Return): For US citizens, permanent residents (Green Card), and visa holders who meet the IRS Substantial Presence Test (SPT).
  • Form 1040-NR (Non-Resident Return): For international students (F-1/OPT/CPT), scholars (J-1), and short-term non-immigrants with US-sourced wages or stipends.
  • Dual-Status Returns: For taxpayers who were both non-residents and residents during the same calendar year.
  • All 50 State Returns: Resident, non-resident, and part-year state tax returns accurately apportioned to prevent double taxation.

Who Needs This Service?

Our 1040 & 1040-NR service is specifically engineered for professionals, foreign nationals, and families seeking maximum refunds and zero compliance surprises:

  • Tech professionals on H-1B, L-1, and O-1 visas with complex equity comp (RSUs, ISOs, ESPP).
  • F-1 students and OPT/CPT graduates claiming US tax treaty exemptions (e.g. US-India Treaty Article 21(2)).
  • Remote workers and consultants with income earned across multiple states.
  • Taxpayers with foreign bank accounts, cryptocurrency trades, and rental properties.
