Comprehensive Business Entity Solutions

Whether you are incorporating an IT consulting company, an e-commerce brand, or a medical practice, choosing the right entity structure and executing accurate annual returns determines how much profit stays in your business.

  • S-Corporation Tax Strategy: Save up to $10,000+ per year in FICA self-employment taxes by splitting income between reasonable salary (W-2) and pass-through distributions.
  • Multi-Member LLCs & Partnerships: Accurately allocate profit/loss percentages and issue compliant Schedule K-1s to partners by March 15.
  • Foreign-Owned US Entities (Form 5472): Avoid the $25,000 failure-to-file penalty for single-member LLCs owned by foreign nationals or non-residents.
