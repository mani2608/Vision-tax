Where is My Tax Refund?

Home Refund Status

Check Federal IRS Refund Status

Track your Form 1040 or 1040-NR directly on the official IRS "Where's My Refund?" portal. Updated once every 24 hours (usually overnight).

Open IRS Refund Portal

What You Need to Check Your Refund:

1. Taxpayer ID
Social Security (SSN) or ITIN

Must match the primary filer on page 1 of your return.

2. Filing Status
Single, MFJ, MFS, or HOH

Select the exact status chosen on your submitted form.

3. Exact Refund Amount
Whole Dollar Value

Found on line 35a of Form 1040 (exclude cents).
