Understanding the 6-Month IRS Tax Extension

Filing an extension gives you additional time to gather late documents (like amended K-1s, 1099-B stock summaries, or foreign records) and properly optimize your deductions without rushing.

  • Extension of Time to File, Not to Pay: If you owe taxes, estimated payment should be made by April 15 to avoid interest. We calculate your safe harbor payment.
  • Both 1040 and 1040-NR: Form 4868 covers both resident and non-resident alien federal returns.
  • Electronic IRS Tracking: You receive an official IRS submission ID confirming your timely filing.
