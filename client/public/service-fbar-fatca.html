FBAR (FinCEN 114) vs. FATCA (Form 8938)

Many filers confuse FBAR and FATCA. Both are mandatory international disclosures, but they are governed by different US government agencies and have separate thresholds.

  • FBAR (FinCEN Form 114): Filed directly with the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (US Treasury). Triggered when aggregate non-US financial accounts exceed $10,000 at any time.
  • FATCA (IRS Form 8938): Filed with your federal tax return Form 1040. Thresholds start at $50,000 for single filers ($100,000 for married filing jointly) on the last day of the tax year.
  • PFIC Guidance (Form 8621): Handling foreign mutual funds, ULIPs, and overseas investment trusts with QEF or Mark-to-Market elections.
