Why F-1 & OPT Students Are Exempt from FICA

Under US tax law, non-resident foreign students holding F-1, J-1, M-1, or Q-1 visas are exempt from paying FICA taxes on wages earned from authorized on-campus employment, CPT, and OPT during their first 5 calendar years in the US.

Often, employer automated payroll systems mistakenly withhold FICA taxes. If this happened to you, you are legally entitled to a 100% refund of all withheld amounts.

  • Step 1 (Employer First): The IRS requires filers to first request a refund and corrected W-2c from their employer. We draft this request letter for you.
  • Step 2 (Direct IRS Claim): If the employer cannot or refuses to issue a refund, we file Form 843 (Claim for Refund) and Form 8316 directly with the IRS Service Center.
